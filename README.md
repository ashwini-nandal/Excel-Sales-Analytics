# Excel Sales & Finance Analytics Project

## 1. Sales Analytics Report

### Project Objectives

- Develop a **Customer Performance Report** to analyze customer-level sales performance.
- Create a **Market Performance vs. Sales Targets Report** to compare actual sales against targets across markets.

### Purpose of Sales Analytics

To help businesses monitor, evaluate, and improve sales activities and overall performance.

### Importance of Sales Data Analysis

- Identify sales trends and patterns.
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Evaluate customer and market performance.

### Business Insights and Applications

- Support customer discount decisions.
- Facilitate customer negotiations.
- Identify potential business expansion opportunities in promising markets.

- [Market Performance vs. Target Report](./Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target%20Report.pdf)

## 2. Finance Analytics Report

### Project Objectives

- Develop Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by fiscal year.
- Analyze monthly financial performance using P&L reports.
- Create market-level P&L reports to evaluate financial performance across markets.

### Purpose of Financial Analytics

To evaluate financial performance, support business decision-making, and communicate financial results effectively to stakeholders.

### Importance of Financial Data Analysis

- Compare financial performance across different periods.
- Support budgeting and financial forecasting.
- Evaluate performance against business goals and benchmarks.

### Business Insights and Applications

- Align financial planning with strategic objectives.
- Monitor profitability across fiscal periods and markets.
- Support informed financial decisions and planning.
